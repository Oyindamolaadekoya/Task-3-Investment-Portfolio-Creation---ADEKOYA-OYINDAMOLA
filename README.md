Overview
This task involved building a hypothetical investment portfolio worth ₹1,00,000 across three different asset classes:

Indian Stocks (35%)
ETFs — Index, Gold & Global (45%)
Cryptocurrencies (20%)

The goal was to create a well-diversified portfolio that balances stability, growth, and high-risk/high-reward opportunities.

Portfolio Philosophy
This portfolio is built on a Core-Satellite Strategy:

🏛️ Core (ETFs — 45%) — Provides stable, diversified market exposure at low cost
📈 Satellite (Indian Stocks — 35%) — Captures growth through India's strongest individual companies
🚀 Speculative (Crypto — 20%) — Provides high upside potential for investors willing to accept higher volatility

Why Each Investment Was Chosen
Indian Stocks

Reliance Industries — India's largest company. Diversified across telecom (Jio), retail and energy. Strong long-term compounding story
TCS — India's top IT exporter earning in USD. Natural hedge against INR depreciation. Consistent dividend payer
Infosys — Attractively valued at PE of 15.39x. Offers 4.09% dividend yield and strong AI services strategy

ETFs

Nippon Nifty 50 BeES — Tracks India's top 50 companies. Lowest cost Nifty ETF with 5-year CAGR of ~11.24%
SBI Gold ETF — Hedge against inflation and INR weakness. Gold outperforms during market stress
Motilal Oswal Nasdaq 100 ETF — Exposure to top US tech companies (Apple, NVIDIA, Microsoft, Meta) through a single Indian instrument

Cryptocurrencies

Bitcoin (BTC) — Most institutionally accepted crypto. Fixed supply of 21M coins. Best risk-adjusted crypto holding
Ethereum (ETH) — Leading smart contract blockchain. Glamsterdam upgrade mid-2026 is next major catalyst
Solana (SOL) — Highest growth potential. AI agent ecosystem and Wall Street payment adoption growing
